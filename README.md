# Metamorphosis

Metamorphosis is a top-down exploration and survival game about transformation, vulnerability, and discovery.

## Big Idea

After mysteriously transforming into a larva, a human must explore their unfamiliar surroundings, uncover the story behind the room, and find a way to survive. Trapped inside a room that now feels impossibly vast, their only hope of escape is to complete their metamorphosis, become a butterfly, and fly out through the window.

## Aesthetic

- Past time
- Fantasy
- Discovery

## Dynamics

- The player must survive through day and night cycles to complete the transformation.
- During the day, the player must avoid light to keep their stress level low.
- The player must avoid loud sounds during both day and night.
- If the stress level gets too high, the character dies.
- The player must find and eat food to survive.
- The player can interact with certain objects to discover parts of the story.

## Mechanics

- Transformation process
- Character movement
- Day and night cycles
- Stress meter
- Hunger system
- Light detection
- Sound detection
- Food collection
- Object interaction
- Death condition

## Game Pillars

- **Survival:** Balance hunger and stress while navigating environmental dangers.
- **Discovery:** Explore the room and interact with objects to uncover its story.
- **Metamorphosis:** Survive long enough to progress from larva to butterfly.

## Goal

The player must survive, discover the story, successfully transform into a butterfly, and escape the room.

## Objectives

- Survive through the day and night cycles.
- Avoid light and loud sounds to manage stress.
- Find and eat food to survive.
- Explore and interact with objects to uncover the story.
- Progress through each stage of transformation.

## Winning Condition

Complete the transformation into a butterfly and escape through the window.

## Visual and Game Direction

The game uses a top-down perspective with a flat visual design. Pixel art may be explored as an optional direction.
